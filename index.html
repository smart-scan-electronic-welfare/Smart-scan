TACTICAL WORKBENCH & METHODOLOGY

Smart Scan System Modules & Operational Workbench

Mission-critical functional modules designed for tactical RF surveillance: operator authentication & access control, forensic search audit history, target watchlists, threat incident alarms, and real-time DSP hardware calibration.

The Conventional Problem

Conventional wideband scanning architectures cycle linearly across frequencies with static dwell times. When hundreds of channels operate simultaneously, high-threat intermittent signals (e.g. burst telemetry, frequency-hopping drones, frequency-agile jammers) are easily missed while valuable DSP cycles are wasted analyzing known commercial FM or TV carriers.

Smart Scan Adaptive Triage

Smart Scan implements a multi-tier triage algorithm. As transmissions are detected, the system calculates an anomaly score based on spectral variance, duty cycle, unknown carrier footprint, and RSSI burst profile. High-priority observations receive up to 8x higher dwell frequency, ensuring zero-packet loss on suspicious transmissions.

STRATEGIC EVALUATION

SWOC Project Analysis

Comprehensive engineering evaluation of the Smart Scan framework across strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and constraints.

Strengths

  • Ultra Low-Cost Prototype: Realized using sub-$35 SDR hardware without requiring multi-thousand dollar spectrum analyzers.
  • Field Portability: Self-contained setup runs entirely on laptop USB bus power for field sweeps.
  • Adaptive Resource Scheduling: Dwell scheduling scales dynamically based on signal risk ratings.
  • Open Modular Stack: Web standards (HTML5/Canvas/JS) enable zero-dependency cross-platform deployment.

Weaknesses

  • ADC Bit Depth Limit: 8-bit dynamic range (~48 dB SNR ceiling) restricts sensitivity in heavy interference.
  • Host Compute Overhead: Dense multi-carrier FFT puts significant load on general-purpose laptop CPUs.
  • Single-Tuner Architecture: Sequential sweeping cannot instantaneously monitor disparate frequency bands simultaneously.

Opportunities

  • TinyML Neural Modulation Classifier: Deploy 1D-CNN directly on edge for instantaneous LoRa / FSK / QPSK modulation identification.
  • Distributed Sensor Mesh: Link multiple low-cost Raspberry Pi nodes to calculate signal TDoA (Time Difference of Arrival) geolocation.
  • Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) Detection: Train signature models to identify proprietary drone control and video links.

Challenges

  • High False Alarm Rate in Urban RF: Distinguishing benign IoT bursts from hostile rogue emitters in congested 2.4 GHz bands.
  • Antenna Matching across Wide Bandwidth: Maintaining uniform VSWR impedance across 500 MHz to 2 GHz without physical reconfiguration.
  • Frequency Hopping Countermeasures: Capturing agile spread-spectrum signals that switch channels faster than the SDR dwell interval.
